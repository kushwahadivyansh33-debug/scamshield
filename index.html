A LITTLE CLARITY GOES A LONG WAY

What we look for.

Scams often follow familiar patterns. This quick check points them out so you can make a more informed choice.

01

Too good to be true

Unexpected prizes, giveaways, or promises of easy money.

02

Pressure to act now

Urgent deadlines designed to stop you from thinking twice.

03

Requests for secrets

Asks for passwords, bank details, or personal information.

04

Unusual links

Link shorteners, strange web addresses, and other URL clues.

A helpful heads-up, not a guarantee. ScamShield uses on-device text patterns and optional English speech recognition for educational purposes. It is not a security scanner, cannot verify whether a message, call, or website is genuine, and may miss scams or flag legitimate content. When in doubt, contact the organization through a channel you find independently.
